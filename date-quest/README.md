# Date #2 invitation 💌

1. "Want to go out with me?": the No button runs away.
2. Yes shows a "Yay 💖! This is the best decision you've ever made." pop-up.
3. She picks a date idea (each one shows a meme), or "Surprise me", or types her own idea.
4. She gets a ticket for Tuesday, Nov 24 · 7:00pm and taps **Send**, which emails you her pick, her note, and how many times she tried to press No.

## Setup
1. **Email key:** go to https://web3forms.com, enter your email, and copy the access key they email you.
   Paste it into `web3formsKey` at the top of `script.js`. Without a key, Send falls back to copying her answer so she can text it.
2. **Date ideas:** edit the `IDEAS` list in `script.js`.
3. **Publish:** GitHub repo → Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → pick this branch, `/ (root)`.
   Link: `https://brianjeong216.github.io/WebDevProject/date-quest/`
